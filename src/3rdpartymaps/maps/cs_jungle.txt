Deep Jungle - Hostage Rescue
by Shiroko (shiroko@dune2k.com)

Counter-Terrorists: Rescue the hostages
imprisoned deep in the jungle in the two
terrorists' "safehouses".

Terrorists: Prevent Counter-Terrorist
force from rescuing the hostages.

Other Notes: There are four hostages
spread out in the houses. This map may
not run smooth on slow machines!!!

Background Story: Two days ago four key
buisnessman from around the world were 
kidnapped by a terrorist organization in 
order to use as a bargin card for releasing
imprisoned terrorists, knowing that the 
pressure and damage to the stock market
will disturb goverments around the world.
Our intelligence traced the location of
the hostages to a rainforest where two
small abandoned houses are used as their
holding place. Intelligence also points
out that the hostages are well guarded and
that the terrorists have AA capabilities so
the rescue force will have to advance on
foot to the target. Up to ten terrorists 
might be guarding the hostages.
